An agent publishes a listing in three calls:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the category schema">
    `describe_category` with no arguments lists the categories. With a category, it returns the attribute fields that category declares, for example `size_eu` and `colorway` for sneakers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the listing">
    `create_listing` takes the core fields (title, price, condition, category and shipping) plus an `attributes` object for the category's fields. Prices are integer cents: `12500` is €125.00.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a photo">
    `add_item_image` takes the photo as base64. **The first photo publishes the listing**, and the result's `visible` says so.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then `my_listings` lists the seller's items, and `update_listing` marks one reserved or sold, changes the price or edits the text.

## Listings need a photo

A listing with no photo is **pending**: it is hidden from search, browse and category counts, and its item page shows placeholder art and is not indexed.

- `create_listing` with no images still succeeds. The result carries `photo_required`, `visible: false` and `next_steps` pointing at `add_item_image`.
- The first photo publishes the listing; no second call is needed.
- Deleting the last photo hides the listing again.
- Pending listings carry `needs_photo: true` everywhere a listing is returned.

The owner can also add, delete and reorder photos on the item page itself. Design record: [ADR 0005](https://github.com/thingery/thingery/blob/main/docs/adr/0005-photo-gated-visibility.md).

## Photos

- Up to 5 MB decoded, as JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF. A data-URL prefix is accepted.
- The server re-encodes every image: it strips all metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP), applies the orientation, and downscales anything larger than 2000 px.
- Animated GIFs are stored as stills. Large PNGs may be rejected, so prefer JPEG.

## Attributes

Declared attributes are validated: their types, required fields and allowed values. Keys a category does not declare are still accepted and searchable. See [Data conventions](/reference/data-conventions#flexible-schema).

## Payouts

To get paid through Thingery with buyer protection, a seller finishes Stripe onboarding with `get_payout_setup`. It is optional: without it, items can still be bought as off-platform orders. See [Orders](/guides/orders).
