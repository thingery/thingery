An agent publishes a listing in three calls:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the category schema">
    `describe_category` with no arguments lists the categories. With a category, it returns the attribute fields that category declares, for example `size_eu` and `colorway` for sneakers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the listing">
    `create_listing` takes the core fields (title, price, condition, category and shipping) plus an `attributes` object for the category's fields. Prices are integer cents: `12500` is €125.00.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a photo">
    `add_item_image` takes the photo as base64. **The first photo publishes the listing**, and the result's `visible` says so.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then `my_listings` lists the seller's items, and `update_listing` marks one reserved or sold, changes the price or edits the text.

## Single-player mode

Thingery works with zero buyers: catalog items now, sell them later. The same three tools above plus two more cover the whole loop.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft from a photo or one line">
    `draft_listing` turns one photo (base64, max 5 MB) and/or a one-line description into a **proposal**: title, category, condition, brand, registry-validated attributes, per-field confidence, a fair-market-value range from real comparable listings, and `open_questions` to confirm with the seller. Nothing is created or published — review the draft, then call `create_listing` yourself. It costs one LLM call per draft on the operator's account (10 drafts/hour/seller, plus a deployment-wide daily budget; exhausted budgets fail closed until the next UTC day, and a text-only draft is the cheaper path). If the deployment has no extraction key configured, the call fails closed — identify and price the item manually instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save to private inventory">
    `create_listing` accepts `status: "stored"` (default `"active"`). A stored item is your private inventory: buyers, search and other agents never see it — `get_item` answers `not_found` to anyone but the owner, and it is excluded from buyer-alert matching. You see your stored items with `my_listings` (optionally `status: "stored"`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip to for-sale">
    `update_listing` with `status: "active"` puts a stored item on the market instantly — photos, attributes, price and search indexing are all preserved, so there is no re-entry. Buyer alerts fire on the flip. Transition rules: only an `active` item can be stored; a stored item flips to `active` or `deleted` only; a live order blocks storing (typed errors `invalid_item_transition` / `item_in_order`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export listing copy">
    `format_listing` turns one of your items into ready-to-paste copy for Subito, Vinted, Wallapop or eBay — a title clamped to the platform limit, a structured description with a market-range line, platform-labeled fields, and a versioned JSON envelope for API use. `language`: `"it"` (default) or `"en"`. Deterministic and export-only: nothing is posted anywhere; you paste the copy yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Draft photo privacy

When a draft is created, the photo and text are sent to the LLM provider configured by this Thingery deployment solely to produce the structured draft. Photos are stripped of metadata (EXIF/GPS) and downsampled before they leave the server, and inputs are not stored: nothing is retained from the extraction call — no images, no model conversation. Only the listing the seller explicitly confirms and publishes is saved. Design records: [ADR 0010](https://github.com/thingery/thingery/blob/main/docs/adr/0010-stored-status.md) covers the `stored` status; the draft extraction pipeline is documented in the [tool reference](/tools/listings).

## Listings need a photo

A listing with no photo is **pending**: it is hidden from search, browse and category counts, and its item page shows placeholder art and is not indexed.

- `create_listing` with no images still succeeds. The result carries `photo_required`, `visible: false` and `next_steps` pointing at `add_item_image`.
- The first photo publishes the listing; no second call is needed.
- Deleting the last photo hides the listing again.
- Pending listings carry `needs_photo: true` everywhere a listing is returned.

The owner can also add, delete and reorder photos on the item page itself. Design record: [ADR 0005](https://github.com/thingery/thingery/blob/main/docs/adr/0005-photo-gated-visibility.md).

## Photos

- Up to 5 MB decoded, as JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF. A data-URL prefix is accepted.
- The server re-encodes every image: it strips all metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP), applies the orientation, and downscales anything larger than 2000 px.
- Animated GIFs are stored as stills. Large PNGs may be rejected, so prefer JPEG.

## Attributes

Declared attributes are validated: their types, required fields and allowed values. Keys a category does not declare are still accepted and searchable. See [Data conventions](/reference/data-conventions#flexible-schema).

## Payouts

To get paid through Thingery with buyer protection, a seller finishes Stripe onboarding with `get_payout_setup`. It is optional: without it, items can still be bought as off-platform orders. See [Orders](/guides/orders).

## Agent skill

The [`sell-item` skill](https://github.com/thingery/thingery/blob/main/skills/sell-item/SKILL.md) walks an agent through the whole pipeline: photos, identifying the item, pricing, publishing — or saving to private inventory and exporting platform copy.
