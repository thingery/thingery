What Thingery is — and is not

Thingery is a free, open-source service for listing and finding second-hand goods, designed to be used by people directly and by AI assistants acting for them. Browsing and searching needs no account; listing and ordering need one.

Thingery is a venue. We host listings, track orders between buyers and sellers, and can process payments (see below). We do not own, inspect, or warrant the goods, and we are not a party to the contract of sale between buyer and seller — that contract is between the two of you.

Orders and payments

A buyer and a seller make an order together, and its status is tracked until it is paid out, refunded, or cancelled. Payment works in one of two ways:

  • Direct (off-platform). The two of you agree on payment yourselves — cash at pickup, a bank transfer, whatever you choose. The seller confirms receipt in Thingery, and the order completes. Thingery cannot reverse an off-platform payment; sort problems out between yourselves.
  • On-platform (Stripe). Checkout runs on Stripe's payment page: your card details go to Stripe and never touch Thingery's servers. The payment is held by the platform's Stripe account until the item is delivered and the dispute window has passed, then paid out to the seller's connected Stripe account (minus Stripe's fees). Cancelling a paid order refunds the buyer through Stripe.

If something goes wrong with an on-platform order, the buyer can open a dispute during the dispute window (48 hours after delivery by default). Payout is held while a Thingery operator looks at the order and decides the outcome. Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically after 24 hours; delivered orders pay out automatically after the dispute window.
