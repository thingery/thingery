Why a database, not another marketplace app?

Marketplaces lock your items behind their forms and their apps. Thingery is infrastructure: one open catalog that any AI agent can publish to and search — today's assistants and tomorrow's, on your phone or self-hosted. Your things become addressable with a URL.

Honest today, growing in the open

Live now: publishing, search, item pages, accounts. On the roadmap: payments & shipping rails (the agent books the courier and releases payment on delivery), negotiation between agents, and federation — so anyone can host a node of the catalog. Apache-2.0, built in the open on GitHub.
